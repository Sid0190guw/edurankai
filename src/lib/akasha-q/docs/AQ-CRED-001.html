AQ-CRED-001 — Twelve-month credibility timeline and gate mapping

PURPOSE AND STANDING

This document maps the four credibility bands defined in AQ-NODE-G0 onto programme gates AQ-GATE-04 through AQ-GATE-08, names the evidence that opens each gate, and fixes the public position permitted while a band is current. It constrains what the Akasha-Q programme may state during its first twelve funded months. It places no constraint on what may be designed or built in that period: the full reach ladder — optical bench, metro fibre and free-space, 20–30 km HAPS relay, CubeSat, operational LEO satellite, constellation — remains the programme's stated intent throughout, and the first-band restriction on claims is not a restriction on architecture.

At the time of writing nothing has been built and no element has reached laboratory validation. Every row below describes an intended design or a defined process. Present evidence level across L0–L9 is Concept, with Theory in preparation. The programme is funded from EduRankAI revenue and disciplined reinvestment; external capital, partnership and collaboration change the rate at which the bands below are traversed and never the evidence required to leave one.

THE ADVANCEMENT RULE

RULE AQ-CRED-R1

Evidence unlocks a level. Elapsed time never does. No gate opens on a calendar date. A gate opens when its named artefacts exist, are recorded in the Evidence & Provenance Fabric (L6), and survive review against DC-1 (every value tagged MEASURED / DERIVED / MODELLED) and DC-5 (the transition is reconstructable from the Fabric alone). Bands describe where the programme is expected to be; a band carries no authority to advance a claim.

MONTH-11 AT DM-2

A programme sitting at DM-2 in month 11 is in an accurate state, not a failing one. The failing state is a programme holding DM-2 evidence while making a DM-4 statement. Under DC-2 the absence of an artefact is itself a value: an unmet gate is recorded as undetermined with the missing artefact named, never as "on track" and never as a benign default.

WHAT UNLOCKS EACH GATE

  • AQ-GATE-04 — design record admitted. L0–L9 layer definitions written as testable statements; DC-1 to DC-5 expressed so that each has a falsifying observation; trust boundaries TB-1 to TB-5 drawn on the AQ-NODE-01 block diagram with the TB-4 hardware gate identified as a physical part; conflicts CF-1..CF-7, failure modes F-1..F-14 and decision boundaries B-1..B-5 registered with a named owner and a current state. Opens: publication of architecture.
  • AQ-GATE-05 — model admitted. An executable model of AQ-TSE-01 covering NORMAL, RESTRICTED, CRITICAL, DENY and RECOVERING; DC-4 shown against a fault-injection set derived from F-1..F-14, with trust falling on a single observation and rising only through the full recovery sequence; command authority evaluated so that Q1–Q3 act as hard constraints and Q4 is shown, over the whole run set, only ever to reduce what Q1–Q3 allowed; abstain and safe-hold demonstrated as reachable outcomes rather than declared ones. Opens: statements about simulated behaviour under a named assumption set.
  • AQ-GATE-06 — bench instrumentation admitted. L1 sensing and entropy acquisition running on real hardware inside TB-2 with deterministic timestamping; an uncertainty budget written and filed before the first measurement run; every published quantity carrying its DC-1 tag. Opens: statements about one measured quantity on one bench.
  • AQ-GATE-07 — reconstruction admitted. A reviewer with no access to the operators reconstructs each consequential transition from the Evidence Fabric alone (DC-5), including at least one deny and one abstain; the TB-4 hardware gate shown to hold against a commanded physical effect; the correction log open to that reviewer. Opens: statements about the trust chain, not only about instruments.
  • AQ-GATE-08 — witnessed repetition. The Band C procedure repeated on separate days by a second operator under external observation, with dispersion reported in the quantity's own units and the assumption set restated at each run. Opens: statements that a capability is repeatable on this bench.

RECORD AND REVIEW

Each gate decision is written to the Evidence Fabric as a single entry: gate identifier, artefacts presented with their Fabric ids, reviewer, outcome from the permitted set (execute, execute-restricted, safe-hold, abstain, deny), and the specific artefact whose absence produced an abstain. Gate review may return abstain — insufficient confidence to authorize — and that outcome is filed, published to the claim register, and carries no penalty. L8 analysis may summarise a gate pack but never issues the outcome; a named human does. Bands are re-baselined at each gate opening, so a gate opened in month 4 does not compress the remaining bands and a gate unopened in month 12 rolls forward with its missing artefact still named.
