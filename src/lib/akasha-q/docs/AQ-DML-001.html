AQ-DML-001 — Demonstration maturity ladder DM-0 to DM-6

PURPOSE

This document defines seven demonstration maturity levels, DM-0 to DM-6. The ladder governs what may be shown, to whom it may be shown, what may be said about it afterwards, and what must be true before the next level is claimed. It is a defined process. No element of Akasha-Q has reached Lab validation, and no demonstration above DM-0 has taken place; every level below is a specification of a future act, not a record of a past one.

The ladder is independent of the six-rung reach ladder and of the Part 12 evidence ladder. The three are orthogonal axes. A position on one never implies a position on another, and the most common misuse of this document will be to treat them as a single scale.

CLAIM SCOPE TUPLE

A DM level is never a property of the programme, a layer or a technology. It attaches to (article, reach rung, claim, evidence level, date of record). The sentence “Akasha-Q is at DM-4” is malformed and is corrected wherever it appears, including in material the programme did not write, because an uncorrected malformed level becomes the level everyone quotes.

LADDER INVARIANTS

  • IV-1 A level attaches to the tuple above, never to the programme as a whole.
  • IV-2 A demonstration cannot exceed the evidence level of its weakest load-bearing element. If one input on the path to the outcome is MODELLED, the whole demonstration is bounded at Simulation regardless of how much of the remainder is MEASURED.
  • IV-3 Observers do not set the level. A large public audience does not raise it; an empty room does not lower it. Evidence and observation conditions set it.
  • IV-4 Degradation is monotonic without re-establishment. Any change to the article, protocol or assumption set returns the affected claim to DM-1 until the sequence is re-run. This mirrors DC-4 and is not a formality to be waived for a minor change; the judgement that a change is minor is exactly the judgement DC-4 exists to distrust.
  • IV-5 abstain and deny are results. A demonstration in which the article refused to act is recorded, presented and counted with the same prominence as one in which it acted.
  • IV-6 L8 AI-assisted analysis is advisory only at every level and may never be the evidence on which a level advances.
  • IV-7 A level transfers to nothing. DM-n on the optical bench rung says nothing about metro fibre, HAPS relay, CubeSat, LEO or constellation. Each rung begins again at DM-0.

THE DM-4 HAZARD

OBSERVATION IS NOT VALIDATION

DM-4 is where programmes of this class conflate observation with validation, and they do it in good faith. An observer choosing the inputs feels like a test: the programme did not pick the case, the outcome was not rehearsed, and the room experiences the result as proof. It is not proof. A third party who selects ten cases has exercised ten cases. Every failure class not selected remains entirely unexercised, and under DC-2 the absence of a failure in an unexercised class is undetermined, never benign. The observer's selection is not a sample; it is a selection, and its coverage is unknown to both parties.

THE WORD RULE

No DM-4 result may use the word validated, or any inflection of it, in material prepared for any audience — internal note, reviewer pack, funding conversation, partner briefing, press response or public page. Also prohibited at DM-4: verified, proven, confirmed, shown to be safe. Permitted verbs: observed, recorded, exercised, selected, reproduced under the same script. The prohibition is on the word appearing, not merely on the intention behind it, because the word is what survives into the reader's summary after the qualification around it is discarded. A single audience-specific relaxation of this rule voids the DM-4 record for every audience.

GOVERNANCE OF THE LEVEL

  • The L9 systems engineering and safety governance function is the only authority that records a DM level. A level not written into the L9 record does not exist, whatever occurred in the room and whoever witnessed it.
  • No level is awarded on the day of the demonstration. The record is written after the capture is reconciled against the article log, so that the reaction in the room cannot become part of the evidence.
  • Any deliverer, evidence steward or independent executor may refuse a level. Refusal requires a written reason; it does not require anyone's agreement, and it is not overridden by seniority.
  • Demotion under IV-4 is recorded with the same prominence as promotion, in the same register, with the same distribution. A demotion is not a failure of the programme; an unrecorded demotion is.
  • Where material is prepared for different audiences, the DM level and its full tuple appear in identical wording in all of them. Audience-specific phrasing of a level is prohibited, and its detection is grounds for withdrawing the level.
  • Funding context does not enter the ladder. External capital, partnership and government collaboration accelerate the rate at which levels are attempted; they never lower the evidence required to record one.
