AQ-EVIDENCE-WALL — Claim register: format, rules and operation

STANDING AND SCOPE

The Evidence Wall is the register of every statement Akasha-Q is permitted to make about itself to a reader outside the immediate working group. It is not a summary of the programme and not a presentation asset. It is a controlled list of sentences, each bound to an evidence level and to a navigable path into the L6 Evidence & Provenance Fabric. Nothing appears on the wall except through AQ-PUBLIC-CLAIM-GATE; nothing stays on the wall that cannot be walked to its primary record by a person who did not write it.

The wall exists because the programme's honest position is asymmetric: the intended capability is large and the accumulated evidence is small. No element of Akasha-Q has reached laboratory validation. The highest evidence level currently occupied on the wall is Theory, with a small number of Concept entries; the rungs Lab validation, Field validation, Dynamic platform, Flight/space and Operational demonstration are defined, published on the wall, and deliberately empty. An empty rung is information (DC-2): it is displayed, not hidden.

THE SEVEN-FIELD ENTRY

Every entry carries exactly seven fields. A field may not be left blank; where nothing is known the field carries the explicit token UNDETERMINED with a one-line statement of what would determine it.

EVIDENCE LEVEL MOVEMENT

Level movement follows DC-4. A level falls on a single adverse observation — a failed re-run, a discovered model error, a withdrawn artefact — and the fall is recorded the same working day without a meeting, by the custodian, on the report of anyone in the programme. A level rises only through the full sequence: artefact produced, independent navigation audit passed, technical review at the gate, disposition recorded. There is no provisional or pending upgrade state; between the two, the entry sits at its lower level.

THE THREE-STEP NAVIGATION RULE

A claim may remain on the wall only while a non-author can reach its primary record in three steps or fewer, unaided, from the entry itself.

Removal is not falsification. A removed claim may be true; the finding is only that the programme cannot currently show why it should be believed. Removed entries return to the gate at stage S1 and may be re-posted unchanged once the path exists. Removal is executed by the custodian on the navigator's finding and is not subject to panel override — a blocked path is cured by fixing the trace, never by argument.

AQ-CL-0018 — Struck on 2026-08-14 by the custodian on the report of the simulation author.

Claim (as posted)In simulation of the AQ-NODE-01 acquisition path under channel model CM-2, the L2 timing residual stays inside the budget stated in AS-7.
Evidence level (as posted)Simulation — tier CA
Strike reasonThe harness that produced SIM-L2-007 instantiated a disciplining reference that the CA architecture does not contain. The run is valid as a run; it is a model of a node other than the one the claim names. The claim was therefore never entailed by its own trace.
DispositionEntry retained on the wall, struck through, with the strike record navigable at step 1 (AQ-EB-0018-strike). Not deleted, not silently edited.
Reissued asAQ-CL-0018R — Undetermined at Concept: "The CA architecture defines an L2 timing residual budget whose bound is undetermined pending a disciplining-source decision; no simulated or measured residual is claimed."

Struck-claim handling. A strike is permanent and public within the register. The struck sentence remains legible so that a reader who saw it can find out what happened to it; the strike record names the reason, the date, the artefact at fault and the reissued claim id if any. A struck sentence may not be resubmitted verbatim. Any paraphrase of a struck sentence is treated as a resubmission of it and is refused at admissibility.

OPERATING NOTE

An entry that has not been navigated within one full review cycle is not assumed still good. It is marked UNCONFIRMED and displayed as such until a navigator clears it. Absence of a recent audit is a value, not a default to benign (DC-2).
