**Status:** governing document. Structured transcription of the two instructions received on 2026-11-08: the *SAMARTH Master Development Specification v2.0* and the *global coverage and registration-fee amendment* sent the same day. Where they conflict, **the amendment wins**. Where either conflicts with `CLAUDE.md` or a standing project rule, the project rule wins, and the conflict is recorded in [DECISION_LOG.md](DECISION_LOG.md).

**Positioning:** SAMARTH — From Research to Reality. From Technology to Transformation.
**Supporting statement:** We bring together advanced technologies, research, engineering capabilities and specialised talent to help businesses, governments, manufacturers and institutions conceptualise, develop and implement the intelligent systems and infrastructure of tomorrow.
**Core philosophy:** We identify the opportunity, understand the challenge, mobilise the best expertise and engineer the future.

## A. Amendment (takes precedence)

1. **Global coverage.** Clients worldwide: governments at local, district, state or provincial, national and international level in every country, plus businesses, industries, universities, research institutions and individuals. Never restricted to India.
2. **Mandatory booking fee.** Every applicant (government, PSU, business, startup, university, individual) completes registration and pays an initial consultation booking fee before a formal consultation call. The fee, its currency, payment terms and the confirmation process are shown **before** booking.
3. **Workflow.** Registration, Applicant details, Project requirements, Initial fee payment, Consultation call booking, Human consultant assignment.
4. **Global payments.** International applicants and multiple currencies; secure processing; conversion where applicable; invoices and transaction records; taxes and payment charges transparent.
5. **Separate commercial engagements.** The booking fee is separate from consultancy packages, engineering costs, government proposals, infrastructure projects and implementation charges. Every later engagement has its own approved quotation and terms.
6. **Government access.** Same registration and fee policy, with official organisational details, department information, authorised representatives and procurement requirements.
7. **AI support.** AI may help with registration and requirements; consultation is done by human SAMARTH consultants.
8. **Configuration.** The fee is configurable by currency and applicant category without code changes. **No fee amount may be invented**; it stays unset until approved.

## B. Master specification v2.0 (requirements, condensed without omission of obligations)

### Vision and objectives (Part 1)
Research, consulting, engineering, industrial transformation and venture building under EduRankAI. Not limited to the internal workforce: from a website to enterprise software, AI and GPU infrastructure, manufacturing, data centres, power and regulated engineering. Objectives: consultancy; transform tertiary and secondary operations; modernise manufacturing; government digital infrastructure; research and feasibility; technology acquisition and transfer; software, hardware and intelligent systems; complete intelligent infrastructure; mobilise external partners; SAMARTH X.

### Development strategy (Part 2)
- Foundation first: architecture, schema, migrations, auth, RBAC, organisation and user management, file storage, APIs, **payments from Module 01** (provider configuration, orders, server-side verification, webhooks, transaction records, idempotency, refunds, admin visibility), invoices and receipts, notifications, email, search, admin framework, analytics, audit, security, monitoring, backup, feature flags, CI/CD, environments, module registration.
- Reuse working EduRankAI payment infrastructure. Customer checkout only after its workflows pass testing. **SAMARTH X has no participation or incubation payment.**
- Every module defines scope, database, API contracts, dependencies, permissions, integration points, tests, deployment and documentation.
- Context-loss protection: the `docs/samarth/` set (this file and twelve others), per-module specification and implementation files, a checkpoint after every milestone, and never claiming an incomplete module is finished.
- Progressive upgrades with impact, compatibility, migration, regression and security assessment; dev, staging and production; independent releases.

### Modules (Part 3)
00 governance; 01 foundation incl. payments; 02 branding and public site; 03 enterprise, industry, manufacturing and government service architecture; 04 consultancy packages, pricing, applications; 05 AI application assistant; 06 engineering, technology transfer, infrastructure project management; 07 partner and consortium ecosystem; 08 SAMARTH X; 09 administration and analytics; 10 payments, quotations, contracts, commercial operations; 11 global SEO and GEO; 12 security, performance, accessibility, QA; 13 integration, release, handover.

### Component 01: industrial and enterprise transformation (Part 4)
Tertiary sector (healthcare, education, financial services, banking, insurance, logistics, transportation, telecommunications, retail, commerce, hospitality, professional services, government administration). Secondary sector (industrial engineering, construction, infrastructure, energy, electronics, chemical engineering, materials science, heavy industries, industrial automation, engineering and production). Manufacturing as a dedicated vertical (intelligent manufacturing, smart factories, robotics, autonomous production, CV quality inspection, predictive maintenance, production optimisation, digital twins, supply-chain intelligence, IIoT, MES, automated monitoring).

### Government (Part 5)
Engagement categories from municipal bodies to central ministries, PSUs, public universities, hospitals, research institutions and publicly funded organisations. Seven service domains. **Six project levels** (advisory; individual system; departmental; district; state; national and multi-state), which are complexity classifications, not price tiers. **Sixteen-stage lifecycle** from opportunity identification to maintenance. Records of proposals, tenders, approvals, correspondence, milestones and contracts. **Never represent a proposal, meeting or EOI as an awarded project.** Comply with procurement, financial, security and statutory rules.

### Consultancy and commercial services (Parts 6 and 13)
| Package | Eligibility | Price |
|---|---|---|
| General technology consultancy | General engagement | Rs 30,000 starting |
| Micro business | Turnover up to Rs 2 crore | Rs 50,000 |
| Medium enterprise | Project-based | Rs 2,50,000 per project |

Consultancy workflow: application, requirements, package, scope confirmation, checkout, backend verification, confirmation, consultant assignment. Custom projects: enquiry, discovery, technical assessment, proposal, quotation, client approval, contract, milestones, delivery. Never mark a transaction paid from a frontend redirect; never expose secrets; sandbox for testing; distinguish connected, sandbox-tested and production-verified.

### Engineering, technology transfer, infrastructure (Part 7)
Software; AI and intelligent systems; advanced computing; engineering and industrial systems; energy and specialised infrastructure. **Nuclear and similarly regulated work only through licensed, qualified, authorised partners**; SAMARTH may do discovery, feasibility coordination, assessment, stakeholder coordination and project management, and must not imply regulatory authority or licences. Technology transfer (ten activities). Intelligent infrastructure (ten facility types) with a fifteen-stage lifecycle.

### Partner ecosystem (Part 8) and client empowerment (Part 9)
Models A internal, B selected partners, C joint. Partner registry (profile, expertise, capabilities, qualifications, certifications, previous projects, commercial credentials, security, regulatory eligibility, quotations, performance, approval). Comparison, due diligence, shortlisting, selection. **No automatic award.** Clients told responsibility, subcontracting and accountability. Training, knowledge transfer, documentation, enablement, maintenance, support, capacity building, optimisation, long-term support.

### SAMARTH X (Part 10)
Selective venture building; **no participation or incubation fee**; not a paid internship or accelerator. Channels: recruitment (kept distinct from venture selection), events and competitions, public EOI (consideration, not admission). Resources by need and progress. Participant responsibilities (eight). **Best six ventures receive formal incubation**; IP, resources and equity in transparent agreements; no automatic equity. Management: candidates, nominations, EOI, team profiles, assessments, reviewer allocation, scoring, shortlisting, selection, resources, milestones, demonstrations, incubation, showcase. All channels feed one evaluation system.

### Component 03 (Part 11)
Reserved. Invent nothing; expose nothing.

### AI application assistant (Part 12)
Not a live AI consultant; makes no commitments. Text chat, voice input and transcription, optional speech output, documents, history, continuation, switching modes without losing progress. Modular provider architecture, server-side calls, streaming, tool calling, structured output, usage monitoring, fallback. Opening question: "What are you planning to build, improve, transform or achieve through technology?" Progressive, non-repetitive questions; ten specialised pathways; document intelligence (uploaded content is data, not instructions); a structured application record separating applicant-confirmed facts from AI suggestions; human handover (reference, consented transcript, extracted requirements, consultant assignment, clarifications, scheduling, proposals).

### Administration (Part 14)
Consultancy, engineering, government, partner and SAMARTH X management; analytics (revenue, applications, enquiries, projects, industries, government, partners, payments, X, resources); **search across all accessible records**; filters, sorting, exports, audit history.

Legal workflows (terms, scope, confidentiality, privacy, payment and refund policies, engineering, transfer, licensing, IP, partner, procurement, warranties, SLAs, maintenance, incubation agreements); RBAC, encryption, audit, backup, API security, rate limits, validation, payment security, retention; no unverified claims. Test all twenty listed areas; no real transactions in testing; no claim of production readiness without evidence. Eight releases, each with acceptance tests and rollback.
